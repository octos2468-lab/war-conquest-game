## Army System

In the Army System, players can manage their armies through dual progression trees, allowing for diverse strategies in unit upgrades. Each unit can be enhanced through various upgrades that improve their abilities and effectiveness in battles. Players will need to carefully allocate resources to achieve a balanced army.

### Unit Upgrades

Players can upgrade their units to enhance their skills, health, and damage output. Each upgrade requires a specific type of currency, which can be earned through gameplay or purchased.

## Hero System

The Hero System empowers players with unique characters that have special abilities and upgrades. As players progress, they can upgrade their heroes using resources from their exploits in the game.

### Hero Upgrades

Each hero has a specific pathway for upgrades, which can be centered around enhancing abilities or gaining new powers. These upgrades provide significant advantages in battles and can be pivotal in achieving victory.

### Currencies

The game features multiple currencies that players can use to upgrade both their units and heroes. Players must strategize how to allocate their currencies effectively across the three stages of the game: early game, mid-game, and late game.

### Interaction Across Game Stages

The synergy between the Army System and Hero System is crucial throughout the game. As players progress through the three game stages, they will find that their choices in upgrading units and heroes directly impact their overall effectiveness and strategy in combat.
